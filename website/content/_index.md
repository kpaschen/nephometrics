My company offers assistance in planning, creating and maintaining cloud 
computing setups.

Below is a (non-exhaustive) list of things I can do for you. If you want to know
more, please [get in touch](contact).

**DevOps**
* Configure, modify, fix, extend your **Kubernetes** clusters
* Configure, extend, or troubleshoot your **CI/CD** pipelines
* **Automate** your infrastructure setup (for example with Terraform, Ansible)
* Set up **monitoring and alerting** (for example using Prometheus with Grafana and Alertmanager)

**Architecture**
* Scalability review: how far will your current architecture scale, where are the bottlenecks?
* Troubleshoot performance issues
* Reliability assessment: could your system be more resilient? Could maintaining it take less of your engineers' time?

I have used Google Cloud and AWS, but most of my clients so far have used smaller cloud providers or had on-premise clusters. I have experience with baremetal Kubernetes clusters and with airgapped installations.
